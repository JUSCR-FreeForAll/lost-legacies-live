## B. Three valuation approaches

### Cost / replacement
Record actual paid invoices, contractor quotes, verifiable hours, materials, tooling, and realistic replacement scope. Separate sunk cost from future replacement cost. Do not assume development cost equals market value.

### Income
For each use case estimate attributable license revenue, adoption probability, time to market, churn, support cost, maintenance, legal clearance, commercialization cost and discount rate. Show low/base/high cases and identify each assumption. Avoid counting revenue from overlapping components twice.

### Market
Record comparable transactions only where rights, asset type, maturity, territory, exclusivity, term, distribution and royalty base are sufficiently similar. Note whether published data is asking price, signed deal, or actual payment. A broker opinion is not a completed transaction.

Only signed and paid transactions are realized transaction evidence. Proposals and verbal interest are not revenue.

## D. Risk adjustments

For each asset assess chain-of-title uncertainty, missing contributor assignments, open-source restrictions, third-party content, unfinished implementation, lack of customers, security issues, patent prior art, public disclosure, maintenance burden, and rights disputes. Explain the adjustment; do not hide it in a single arbitrary percentage.
