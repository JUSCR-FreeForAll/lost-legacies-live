**Status:** Working draft; not a legal opinion, appraisal, offer, or proof of ownership

## Executive decision

Use a portfolio-holding structure only after counsel confirms chain of title and tax consequences. The preferred commercial model is a non-exclusive, non-transferable, field-of-use and territory-limited license of specifically identified assets. Do not offer the entire portfolio as one undifferentiated asset. Start with a paid, time-limited evaluation of a verified, separable software component; convert interested evaluators to a commercial license.

No rights are granted by this document. No prospective licensee may use, copy, modify, distribute, train on, sublicense, or commercialize any material without a separately signed agreement.

## 2. Rights and provenance gate

For every asset, record creator(s); employment/contractor status; creation dates; source repository and immutable commit; original files; third-party/open-source components; art/music/font/data licenses; signed assignments; existing grants; public disclosures; registrations; trademark/domain status; and known disputes.

Classify each item:
- **Green:** chain of title documented; third-party rights cleared; license scope defined.
- **Amber:** plausible rights but missing contributor, source, license or permission evidence. No commercial grant until resolved.
- **Red:** known third-party restriction, disputed ownership, prohibited material, or missing authority. Exclude from offer.

Keep an SBOM and file-level provenance record for software. Do not relicense upstream code. The central repository ADR-002 is proposed, not a legal opinion; it requires file-level review and states that a separate directory/service boundary does not itself remove copyleft obligations. The JUSCR-FreForAll root license was previously reported as placeholder text referring to AGPL-3.0; inspect the live file and upstream notices before reuse.

## 3. Initial product offer — Time Engine evaluation

**Offer:** Time Engine — Technical Evaluation License  
**Target:** software product company, event/auction platform, systems integrator or game-tech studio  
**Scope:** only a named, versioned package/API and listed documentation after source and provenance review.

**Proposed commercial test terms (negotiation hypotheses, not market valuation):**
- Evaluation fee: $500 for a 30-day, non-production evaluation.
- Commercial implementation fee: opening proposal $5,000, adjusted after scope and diligence.
- Ongoing fee: negotiated fixed annual fee or royalty on narrowly defined attributable receipts.
- No exclusivity by default.
- No sublicensing, source redistribution, production deployment, model training, or derivative commercialization during evaluation.
- No production credentials, private keys, customer data or secrets supplied.
- Evaluation ends automatically; no implied commercial rights.

Do not send this offer until there is a demonstrable build, clean dependency/provenance report, owner authority and written scope. If Time Engine is not the cleanest verified component, choose the first Green asset instead.

## 4. Commercial pricing method

Do not present the recorded $1,000,000 estimate as market value, collateral or an appraisal. Maintain separate figures:
1. **Cost/replacement evidence:** documented hours, invoices, contractor quotes, comparable rebuild scope.
2. **Income evidence:** realistic customer use case, pricing, adoption assumptions, attributable revenue, maintenance and support costs.
3. **Market evidence:** genuinely comparable licenses, published deal terms, or qualified broker/appraiser analysis.
4. **Transaction evidence:** paid pilots, signed licenses, renewals, actual royalty receipts.

For each asset calculate low/base/high scenarios. Label every assumption, date and source. Discount forecasts for incomplete code, no customers, uncertain chain of title, dependencies, maintenance burden and market risk. Do not add overlapping component values or count the same revenue twice.

## 5. Standard deal structure — counsel review required

**Grant:** non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable unless expressly approved; limited by field of use, territory, term, version and named deliverables.

**Reserved rights:** all rights not expressly granted remain with licensor. No assignment or implied transfer of copyright, patent, trademark, domain, token, data, or trade-secret ownership.

**Fees:** upfront fee payable before access; milestones only against accepted deliverables; annual minimum/renewal; royalties only where reporting can be audited.

**Royalty definition:** define gross receipts, permitted deductions, taxes, refunds, chargebacks, bundles, affiliates, currency conversion, reporting period, payment date, records retention and audit shortfall remedy. Avoid vague net-profit formulas.

**Improvements:** each party retains background IP. State who owns newly created deliverables, whether either party receives a license-back, and how feedback is treated. No automatic assignment of all improvements to licensee.

**Controls:** confidentiality, access control, no reverse engineering except non-waivable legal rights, security obligations, incident notice, third-party license compliance, no unauthorized AI training, no use of marks outside guidelines.

**Risk allocation:** representations limited to verified matters; disclose exclusions and dependencies; negotiate liability caps and carve-outs; make indemnities proportionate; scope warranties, support and service levels separately.

**Exit:** expiry/termination triggers, cure periods, stop-use, deletion/return of confidential materials, certified deletion, limited archival copy for legal compliance, accrued payment and audit survival.

**Disputes:** governing law, venue, escalation and mediation/arbitration only after counsel reviews enforceability and cost.

## 7. Buyer-facing one-page positioning

**Negative Imagination Space — Independent IP & Technology Licensing Portfolio**

A creator-led portfolio of original interactive entertainment concepts, software systems, technical architecture and narrative worlds available for carefully scoped evaluation and licensing, subject to rights verification.

**Licensing principle:** retain underlying ownership; grant defined commercial use rights to qualified partners.

**Available categories:** interactive software and platform components; game worlds and narrative properties; technical documentation and designs; selected brands and digital properties where rights are confirmed.

**Partner models:** paid evaluation; non-exclusive commercial license; field-of-use license; co-development with defined deliverable ownership; publishing or adaptation license.

**Diligence after qualification:** asset register, source/version evidence, demonstrations, dependency and provenance summary, rights exclusions, and draft term sheet.

**Not represented:** guaranteed performance, issued patents, independently appraised portfolio value, customer traction, completed production systems, or ownership of third-party material.

**Contact:** owner contact details to be supplied by licensor. No contact details should be invented.

## 8. 30-day execution checklist

### Days 1–5 — rights and inventory
- [ ] Identify legal owner and every creator/contributor.
- [ ] Collect signed assignments and work-made-for-hire agreements where valid.
- [ ] Reconcile repositories, branches, commits and project aliases.
- [ ] Inspect LICENSE, NOTICE, dependency and asset terms.
- [ ] Classify every asset Green/Amber/Red.
- [ ] Exclude unresolved items from sales materials.

### Days 6–10 — proof and packaging
- [ ] Select one Green, demonstrable asset for the first offer.
- [ ] Build a clean demo and technical fact sheet.
- [ ] Create SBOM and provenance report.
- [ ] Assemble cost, income and market valuation evidence.
- [ ] Obtain counsel review of ownership and licensing template.

### Days 11–20 — market validation
- [ ] Build shortlist of 15 qualified organizations.
- [ ] Verify each organization's current submission route.
- [ ] Send individually tailored, non-confidential summaries only after owner approval.
- [ ] Offer paid evaluations only under signed terms.
- [ ] Log responses and objections.

### Days 21–30 — deal conversion
- [ ] Qualify interested counterparties and check conflicts.
- [ ] Sign NDA only if justified; disclose minimum necessary material.
- [ ] Negotiate scope, fee, field, term, royalties and exit.
- [ ] Obtain counsel approval and authorized signature.
- [ ] Invoice and confirm cleared payment before access.
- [ ] Record executed license and payment evidence.
