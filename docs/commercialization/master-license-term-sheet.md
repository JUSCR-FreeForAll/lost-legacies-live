# Master IP License — term sheet and drafting checklist

**Draft for U.S. counsel review. Not an executable agreement and does not grant rights.**

## Parties and authority

- Licensee legal name and jurisdiction: [to be completed]
- Authorized signatories and evidence of authority: [to be verified]
- Effective date: [date]

## Licensed materials schedule
For each item specify asset ID, title, version, file/repository/commit identifiers, deliverables, documentation, excluded components, third-party dependencies, marks and permitted media. No generic grant to “the portfolio.”

## Grant
Licensor grants Licensee a limited, non-exclusive, non-transferable, non-sublicensable license during the term, in the territory [ ], solely in the field of use [ ], to [specific acts: evaluate / execute / integrate / distribute defined object code / display defined content]. No rights arise by implication. All ungranted rights are reserved.

## Evaluation option
If used, evaluation lasts [30] days, is non-production, limited to [named users/environments], and prohibits redistribution, sublicensing, public disclosure, model training, commercial use and access by affiliates. Evaluation access begins only after signature and cleared fee. Expiry does not automatically convert to commercial rights.

## Fees and payment
- Evaluation fee: [ ]
- Initial license fee: [ ]
- Annual minimum / renewal fee: [ ]
- Milestone payments and acceptance criteria: [ ]
- Royalty rate and royalty base (if any): [ ]
- Invoice currency, due date, late charge and taxes: [ ]
- Reporting frequency, records retention and audit: [ ]
- No set-off except as expressly agreed.

Royalty base must define gross receipts, permitted deductions, refunds, chargebacks, bundled sales, affiliate transactions, currency conversion, taxes and reporting. Avoid undefined “net profit.”

## Ownership and improvements
Each party retains its pre-existing/background IP. Identify ownership of bespoke deliverables, modifications, feedback, derivative works and jointly developed materials. State any license-back expressly. No assignment of licensor's background IP. No use of licensee materials beyond performance of the agreement.

## Third-party materials
Schedule open-source software, stock assets, fonts, music, datasets, APIs and other third-party materials with applicable terms. Licensor grants no rights it does not own or control. Licensee must comply with listed third-party terms.

## Confidentiality and security
Define confidential information, permitted recipients, safeguards, exclusions, compelled disclosure, duration and return/deletion. Specify security controls, incident notification, access logging and data handling. Never include secrets, private keys or customer personal data in a demo package.

## Restrictions
No unauthorized sublicensing, resale, public posting, source redistribution, trademark use, reverse engineering where lawfully restrictable, removal of notices, or training of AI/ML models on licensed materials unless specifically and separately authorized. Preserve non-waivable statutory rights.

## Representations, warranties and disclaimers
Make only factual representations supported by diligence: authority to sign, known ownership, listed exclusions, and no known conflicting grant after reasonable inquiry. Avoid absolute warranties of novelty, non-infringement, patentability, uninterrupted operation or commercial success. Define any support, maintenance, acceptance and service levels separately.

## Liability and indemnity
Counsel to negotiate proportionate indemnities, defense procedure, mitigation, liability cap, excluded damages and carefully considered carve-outs (e.g. confidentiality, IP misuse, fraud, willful misconduct, payment obligations). Confirm insurance and enforceability.

## Termination and exit
Specify term, renewal, material breach, cure periods, insolvency rights where enforceable, nonpayment suspension, stop-use, return/deletion, certification, transition assistance, accrued fees, audit and surviving clauses.

## Disputes and general
Choose governing law and venue after counsel advice; escalation and optional mediation/arbitration; notices; assignment; force majeure; entire agreement; amendments; severability; waiver; counterparts; electronic signatures; order of precedence.

## Signature and closing checklist

- [ ] Complete licensed-material schedule and exclusions.
- [ ] Clear contributors, open-source and third-party rights.
- [ ] Confirm territory, field, term and exclusivity.
- [ ] Define fee and auditable royalty formula.
- [ ] Agree improvement ownership and license-back.
- [ ] Counsel reviews warranties, indemnities and liability.
- [ ] Both parties sign.
- [ ] Invoice paid and funds cleared.
- [ ] Deliver only the authorized materials and access.
