# Buyer pipeline and outreach kit

**Program:** Negative Imagination Space — IP Licensing & Exchange  
**Purpose:** qualify prospective licensees for cash-paid, rights-limited licenses.  
**Status:** research targets only; no buyer interest or endorsement is implied.

## Prospect qualification criteria
- Has a product/business that plausibly needs the specific asset.
- Can identify a budget owner and technical/creative evaluator.
- Accepts a scoped, non-exclusive license model.
- Has a credible deployment/use case and ability to pay.
- Will sign appropriate confidentiality and evaluation terms.
- Does not require transfer of the entire portfolio or broad rights beyond the business case.

## Pipeline stages
Research → Qualified → Owner approval → Outreach sent → NDA if needed → Paid evaluation proposed → Evaluation active → Term sheet → Counsel review → Signed → Invoice paid/cleared → Access granted → Renewal / royalties.

## Non-confidential first-contact email

Subject: Licensing discussion — [specific asset / business use case]

Hello [name/team],

I’m developing a creator-owned portfolio of interactive software and entertainment properties and am exploring narrowly scoped, non-exclusive commercial licenses.

I thought [specific asset] may be relevant to [specific product/use case] because [one evidence-backed sentence]. I can share a short non-confidential overview and, if there is a fit, discuss a paid, time-limited technical or creative evaluation under written terms.

The proposed structure preserves the underlying owner's rights and limits any license to an agreed field of use, territory, term and deliverables. No confidential materials or source code are included in this initial note.

Would your team be the right group to review a short overview, or is there a preferred submission process?

Regards,  
[Owner's legal name]  
[Approved contact details]

## Outreach rules
- Obtain owner approval before sending.
- Personalize the use case and avoid unsubstantiated claims.
- Do not state the portfolio is worth $1 million.
- Do not claim patents, customers, traction, production readiness or ownership unless evidenced.
- Do not attach source code, private keys, secrets, unpublished patent-enabling details or third-party materials.
- Track consent, opt-outs and applicable email/privacy requirements.
- Do not imply a prospect has expressed interest until they have.
